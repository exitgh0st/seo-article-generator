# Researchers Confirm ExfilSquad's Access to Sensitive Data

## Research Findings

Fortra Intelligence and Research Experts (FIRE) analyzed ExfilSquad's publicly disclosed
data and confirmed the group's access to sensitive information. The researchers stated:
"The leading theory on the initial attack vector that enabled exfiltration is misconfigured
Microsoft Power Page portals that allowed for public read access."

## Attack Mechanism

The breaches involved unauthorized access to Microsoft D365 CRM and ERP instances. Power
Pages, a SaaS platform for creating external-facing business websites, contained a known
weakness. When the Anonymous Users web role receives table permissions, "the table's data
can be read by anyone visiting the site." Fortra identified over 10,000 potentially exposed
Power Pages instances publicly accessible.

## Victims and Data Volume

ExfilSquad claimed 15 victim organizations; 13 data dumps were published August 7 via
torrents, totaling 382.64 GB and 27 million records. Confirmed victims included the City of
Atlanta, UK Department for Education, and UK Police National Legal Database. District of
Columbia Public Schools had 60,000 records leaked containing student names, dates of birth,
and identifiers.
