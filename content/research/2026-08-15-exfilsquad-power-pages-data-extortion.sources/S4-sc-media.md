# ExfilSquad data extortion group linked to 13 victim data leaks

## Victim Count & Timeline

ExfilSquad emerged on July 26, 2026, initially claiming 15 victim organizations. By August
7, the group published data from 13 confirmed victims via torrents.

## Data Volumes

The leaked material totaled 382.64 GB containing approximately 27 million records across
government, education, financial services, and manufacturing sectors.

## Named Victims

- City of Atlanta
- UK Department for Education
- UK Police National Legal Database
- District of Columbia Public Schools (DCPS), released 60,000 censored student records with
  personally identifiable information

## Fortra's Analysis

According to researchers at Fortra Intelligence and Research Experts (FIRE), "data samples
publicly released by the group" were reviewed and their authenticity confirmed.

## Technical Vulnerability

Researchers theorize attackers exploited misconfigured Microsoft Power Pages portals
granting public read access. The weakness stems from "Anonymous Users web role assigned to a
table permission" allowing unauthorized visitors site access. Attackers likely identified
vulnerable organizations through portal crawling targeting these exposed Microsoft D365 CRM
and ERP instances.
